Assembly

  1. Print 3 segments upright + the two door plates (or 3 doors + 2 sliders individually) flat.
  2. Join segments on the bench: flange rings engage, 6× M3 through the joint bosses.
  3. Snap the two scalloped sliders into the rear-notch rails, slid fully open.
  4. Hold the box under the desk (it can sit a few inches off the wall — cable management lives behind it); mark the 4 ear holes with a nail tap.
  5. Hand-drive the 4 pan-head screws through the external ears — sharp-point 4.2×20 screws self-thread into wood, no drill; 20 mm length means the tip stops at half the desk's thickness. Snug, don't overtighten.
  6. Pass power plugs head-first through the rear notch; set the charger and Mac mini on the lattice floor; connect everything.
  7. Slide the scalloped shutters together — they close around the installed cables.
  8. Hang the three fluted doors: hook top, drop, bottom tab seats. Lift-and-tilt to remove any time.
Notes: interior is 15.9 × 5.8 × 3.0 inches — sized for a slim AC→USB-C charger + a Mac mini M4, not for a full-size socket strip. Desk must take #8 wood screws (solid wood/ply). Fully parametric Blender source included — any dimension change regenerates every part.
